## Full description
TextCraft is the fast, clean word counter for writers, students, bloggers and marketers.

Type or paste any text and instantly see:
• Word count and character count (with and without spaces)
• Sentences, paragraphs and letters
• Reading time and speaking time — perfect for speeches and scripts
• Keyword density with top keywords and percentages, ideal for SEO
• Readability score (Flesch Reading Ease) so you know how easy your writing is to read

✨ AI writing check
Tap once to catch spelling, grammar and punctuation mistakes, get clear explanations, and copy a corrected version. Free users get daily AI checks.

🗂️ Saved drafts
Save drafts on your device and pick up where you left off.

🔒 Private by design
No account needed. Counting works completely offline, and your text never leaves your phone unless you run an AI check.

⭐ TextCraft Pro
Unlimited AI writing checks and no ads.

Great for essays, college applications, tweets and captions with character limits, blog posts, SEO content, cover letters and more.
