# Privacy Policy — TextCraft: Word Counter

_Last updated: November 3, 2026_

TextCraft: Word Counter ("the app") is published by ANKHOSTING ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles.

## No account required
The app has no sign-up or login. We do not collect your name, email, or contacts.

## Your text stays on your device
Everything you type or paste, and every draft you save, is processed and stored **only on your device**. Word counts, keyword density, readability and other statistics are calculated offline. Uninstalling the app or deleting a draft removes it.

## AI writing check (optional)
Only when you tap **AI writing check**, the text in the editor (up to 6,000 characters) is sent over an encrypted connection to our server (hosted on Supabase) and forwarded to a third-party AI provider (by default, Fireworks AI) for processing to generate corrections. We do **not** store your text. When the default provider (Fireworks AI) is configured, Fireworks AI states that it does not log or store prompt or generation data for open models (data exists only in volatile memory during the request, and prompt caches may be kept in memory for several minutes) and logs only metadata such as token counts; see https://docs.fireworks.ai/guides/security_compliance/data_handling and https://fireworks.ai/privacy-policy.

To enforce the free daily limit and prevent abuse, our server stores only pseudonymous data: a random, app-generated device identifier with a daily usage count, and a keyed hash of your IP address (never the IP address itself) with a daily request count. To check whether you have TextCraft Pro, the server sends the anonymous RevenueCat app user ID to RevenueCat. Usage counts are kept for about 2 days and then deleted.

## Purchases
TextCraft Pro subscriptions are handled by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and purchase records to confirm your subscription. We never see your payment details. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

## Advertising
Free users see banner ads from Google AdMob. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), a Google consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) is shown before ads are requested, and ads are not requested until consent is handled. We request non-personalized ads, but AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and ad-interaction data to serve ads, measure performance and prevent fraud, and AdMob may also collect diagnostic data (such as crash logs and performance data) to keep its SDK working. See https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads. TextCraft Pro removes ads.

## Children
The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Data deletion
Because there is no account, there is no account to delete. Drafts can be deleted in the app or by uninstalling it. Usage counts are pseudonymous and are automatically deleted after about 2 days. To ask about any data, contact us.
